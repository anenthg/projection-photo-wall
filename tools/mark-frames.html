Mark frames

Open a straight-on photo of your wall.
Your photo stays on this computer; nothing is uploaded.

How

For each frame, click the inside corners of the picture opening (mat included if light should land on it) in this order: top-left, top-right, bottom-right, bottom-left. After 4 clicks the next frame starts. Drag any corner to fine-tune. Then copy the result into site/config.js and copy the photo into site/ under the name shown.
