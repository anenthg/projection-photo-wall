Projector

Scenes

Motion that travels across the whole wall and shows only through the frames. The photo scenes use whatever is in the frames; album photos can be tagged then or now. Move the cursor over the projected picture to draw the lantern, gather the fireflies, bend the thread or scatter the birds.

Press S while projecting to start or stop a scene. To pick one on the wall, press an arrow key (the remote's D-pad) in Play: a scene bar appears; OK plays the highlighted scene.

Before you project
  • Each picture now covers the whole inside of its frame, white mat included, and the mat takes light well. Swap the brown backing cards for matte white paper so the opening is bright too. Brown card dulls every colour.
  • Take out any glass or acrylic and peel the film off the front. It bounces the beam straight back as glare.
  • The projected picture must cover every frame with a little margin: measure the span of your frames and place the projector so its picture is about 10% wider (see docs/hardware.md).
  • Black is the absence of light, so the wall and frames still glow faintly. Dim the room and raise Edge trim to keep light off the black frame edges.
  • Mirror or extend your screen to the projector, press Project on wall, then Align each frame against the real thing.
